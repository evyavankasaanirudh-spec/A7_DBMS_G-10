Insurance Fraud Risk Indicators

Certain claim characteristics may require additional review for potential fraud risk.

Examples of possible indicators include unusually high claim amounts, multiple previous claims, and claims submitted shortly after a policy becomes active.

These indicators do not by themselves establish that a claim is fraudulent.

Claims with potential risk indicators may require additional verification, supporting documentation, or manual review.

Fraud assessment should be performed using the applicable rules, policy information, claim history, and available evidence.
