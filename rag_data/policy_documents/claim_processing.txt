Insurance Claim Processing Procedure

An insurance claim generally passes through several stages.

First, the claim is submitted with the required information and supporting documents.

The claim is then reviewed to verify the policy details, claimant information, incident details, and submitted evidence.

The insurer may request additional information or documents when required.

After assessment, the claim may be approved, rejected, or placed under further review according to the policy terms and claim assessment process.

The final decision depends on the applicable insurance policy conditions and the information available during assessment.
