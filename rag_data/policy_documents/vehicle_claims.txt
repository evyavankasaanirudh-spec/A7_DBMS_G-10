Vehicle Insurance Claim Documentation

For a vehicle accident insurance claim, the policyholder should generally provide the insurance policy details, vehicle registration certificate, driving licence, and details of the accident.

Depending on the circumstances of the claim, additional evidence such as photographs of the damaged vehicle, repair estimates, police reports, and other relevant documents may be required.

The claimant should report the accident to the insurance company as soon as reasonably possible and provide accurate information about the incident.

The claim will be reviewed according to the terms and conditions of the applicable insurance policy.
